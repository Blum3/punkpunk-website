About Us

We are an independent studio creating games focused on alternative movements, marginalized groups, and counter-cultures.

Our games aim for strong immersion through tight, purposeful storytelling, carefully treated subjects, impactful sound design, and gameplay systems that directly support the core theme. Settings are rarely grounded in the present real world; instead we favor dystopian, alternate, or reimagined timelines and spaces.

Our artistic direction embraces punk methods and unconventional production choices, both visually and structurally.

Teknopolis

Teknopolis is a top-down 3D exploration and tactical planning RPG initiating you to the Free-Party world, through a story set in a vast megacity controlled by an elite ruling from the central neigborhood. You play as Dice, a music-driven organizer building a clandestine collective. Trying to revive a repressed music movement, free-parties, you will explore dense and layered districts to scout locations, recruit DJs, secure equipment, and meet strange people. Manage your resources and make strategic choices to increase the chances of success and grow your reputation. Music might spark something inside Teknopolis habitants...
